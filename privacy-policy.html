Privacy policy

Local Gallery (Google Play) and Gallery (sideloaded version) · Effective 31 October 2026

Local Gallery shows the photos and videos stored on your device. It has no accounts, no advertising, no analytics and no crash reporting. Your photos, videos and their details are never uploaded. The only data that leaves your device is described under Maps.

Who is responsible

Norfila
Email: yggdrasil@tuta.com

Norfila does not receive any personal data from the app.

What stays on your device

  • Photos and videos are read through Android's media access so the app can show them. If you allow access to selected photos only, the app sees only those.
  • Photo details stored in the files, such as date, camera, rating and the GPS location where a photo was taken, are read to show details, filters, the map and countries. The app keeps a cache of these details on your device so it does not have to read every file again. The country of a photo is worked out on the device, without an internet service.
  • Albums, favorite filters and settings are stored on your device. Albums only link to your files; nothing is copied.
  • Hidden media (photos in .nomedia and dot folders) is only shown if you turn on the Hidden page. The app then reads the folders you choose in Android's folder picker. The sideloaded version can also search your storage if you allow "All files access". Hidden files are only read.
  • Home-screen widgets show an album's cover and photo count on your home screen. Hidden media never appears there.

The app only changes files when you ask it to: deleting (moved to Android's bin where available), moving to another folder, and saving an edited copy. Android backup is turned off for the app, so its data is not copied to a cloud backup. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data removes everything it stored.

Maps

When you open the Map, the app downloads map images over the internet:

  • Simple map: map data and fonts from OpenFreeMap (openfreemap.org), based on OpenStreetMap.
  • Satellite map: satellite images from EOX IT Services GmbH (cloudless.eox.at).

Like any website, these providers receive your IP address, technical details of the request (such as the app and Android version) and which map areas and zoom levels are loaded. Because the map shows where your photos were taken, the requested areas can hint at those places. Your photos and their coordinates are not sent. Recently loaded map images are cached on your device. Please see the providers' websites for how they handle this data. The rest of the app, including Countries, works without internet.

The app never asks for your current location.

When you share or open something

Sharing a photo, opening it in another app, using it as a wallpaper or tapping "Open in maps" hands the photo or its coordinates to the app you pick. That app's privacy policy then applies.

Permissions

  • Photos and videos (READ_MEDIA_IMAGES, READ_MEDIA_VIDEO, READ_MEDIA_VISUAL_USER_SELECTED; READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE up to Android 12): to show your media.
  • Photo locations (ACCESS_MEDIA_LOCATION): to read the GPS location stored in photos for the map and countries.
  • Storage write (WRITE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE, Android 8–10 only): to delete files you choose to delete.
  • Internet and network state: to load map images.
  • All files access (MANAGE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE, sideloaded version only, optional): to find hidden media anywhere on your storage.

Google Play

If you install the app from Google Play, Google processes data according to its own privacy policy. Norfila receives only anonymous statistics from Google, such as install numbers, and crash reports if you have allowed your device to share diagnostics with app developers.

Children

The app is not specifically aimed at children and collects no personal data from anyone.

Your rights

Under data protection law (such as the GDPR) you have the right to access, correct and delete personal data and to complain to a data protection authority. Because Norfila does not receive data from the app, there is nothing stored with us; for map requests, please contact the map providers. Questions are welcome at the email address above.
